info 1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how {{ company_name }} ("we", "our", or "us"), operating the Flixora AI Sales Automation Platform, collects, protects, uses, and discloses information when business leads, clients, and partners interact with our automated outreach, website audit engine, customer support systems, and official WhatsApp Business channels.

By engaging with our automated messaging channels, submitting your contact details, or receiving an automated website proposal from our system, you acknowledge the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Zero-Spam Commitment: {{ company_name }} maintains a strict zero-spam policy. We only reach out to verifiable business entities regarding digital modernization, responsive website audits, and performance consulting. We provide instantaneous opt-out capabilities on all communications.

dataset 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to conduct tailored B2B website audits and communicate proposals:

  • Public Business Contact Details: Business name, industry category, official contact phone number, public business website address, and location.
  • WhatsApp Messaging Data: Sender phone number, verified WhatsApp profile name, incoming message content, conversation timestamps, and message delivery status receipts provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.
  • Technical Audit Information: Public website performance metrics, mobile viewport responsiveness scores, SSL configuration, and technology stack indicators gathered for the purpose of generating digital proposals.
  • User Preferences: Communication opt-in records, conversation status tags, and language preferences.

chat 3. WhatsApp Business & Meta Cloud API Compliance

Our messaging operations strictly abide by the Meta Platform Terms, WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, and WhatsApp Commerce Policy:

  • Authorized API Integration: All WhatsApp interactions are transmitted exclusively through the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. We do not use unofficial scrapers, unauthorized modified WhatsApp clients, or spoofed devices.
  • Template Pre-Approval: Outbound business-initiated conversations utilize pre-approved Meta WhatsApp templates compliant with regional guidelines.
  • Customer Service Window: Inbound conversations adhere strictly to Meta's 24-hour customer care service window. Free-form AI conversational assistance is offered exclusively while responding to direct inquiries initiated by the user.
  • No Reselling of Contact Information: We never sell, lease, or monetize your phone number, chat records, or business identifiers to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

engineering 4. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  1. To deliver automated website audit summaries and personalized interactive design demos requested by prospective clients.
  2. To answer technical inquiries, pricing questions, feature explanations, and scope clarifications via our Flixora AI Autonomous Assistant.
  3. To connect interested prospects with our human sales engineers and design team when requested.
  4. To monitor system uptime, prevent denial-of-service abuse, and verify message delivery fidelity.
  5. To enforce compliance with legal obligations, prevent fraudulent inquiries, and honor immediate opt-out directives.

hub 5. Third-Party Service Processors

To operate our high-speed, 24/7 AI sales infrastructure, we work with industry-standard, SOC2 and ISO27001-certified cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Cloud API): Used for secure, enterprise message transport and webhook event dispatching.
  • AI Model Inference Providers (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Groq): Used for natural language processing of inquiries. Messages sent to inference engines are ephemeral and not used to train public foundation models.
  • Cloud Hosting & Database (Neon PostgreSQL, Render): Encrypted database and server hosting for storing lead records and conversation transcripts.

inventory_2 6. Data Retention & Archival

We retain business conversation logs and lead audit metrics only as long as necessary to fulfill legitimate commercial consulting obligations, typically for a period of up to 12 months from the date of last customer activity, unless a deletion request is received sooner or required by law.

block 7. Immediate Opt-Out & Stop Messaging

We provide frictionless, automated opt-out mechanisms directly inside WhatsApp and via our web portal:

Instant WhatsApp Opt-Out: Replying with any of the following keywords immediately halts all automated outreach and disqualifies future messages:
STOP  •  UNSUBSCRIBE  •  WRONG NUMBER  •  REMOVE ME  •  MAT KARO

Upon receiving an opt-out command, our AI autonomous agent immediately stops messaging, marks your record as disqualified, and suppresses all upcoming automated follow-ups.

gavel 8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction (including under GDPR, California CCPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023), you hold the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information and chat logs we hold regarding your phone number.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to inaccurate business records or contact details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request complete deletion of your records via our dedicated User Data Deletion Page.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we suspend further processing or automated analysis of your web presence.

shield 9. Security Architecture

{{ company_name }} implements bank-grade administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

  • All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • All database storage at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards.
  • API credentials, WhatsApp tokens, and database secrets are managed using encrypted environment variables and masked secret filters.
  • Role-based access controls (RBAC) restrict access to customer conversation histories solely to authorized administrators.
