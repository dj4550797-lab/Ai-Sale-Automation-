stars 1. Core Principles

{{ company_name }} is committed to maintaining the highest messaging quality standards on the WhatsApp platform. We strictly adhere to the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, ensuring that all communications deliver authentic, high-value consulting insights regarding digital web modernization.

check_circle 2. Consent & Opt-In Policy

We value consumer privacy and only initiate outreach under established business-to-business guidelines:

  • Outreach is limited to publicly listed commercial enterprises where modern web architecture, mobile optimization, or website redesign services offer tangible value.
  • Inbound inquiries received on our WhatsApp number are treated as explicit opt-in for our AI sales assistant to answer the prospect's direct queries.
  • We clearly introduce ourselves in our initial greeting: "Hello! I'm Flixora AI, your digital sales and web assistant from {{ company_name }}."

block 3. Immediate Opt-Out & Honoring STOP

Recipients maintain full control over communications. We offer zero-barrier opt-out triggers:

Zero Questions Asked Opt-Out: Replying with words such as STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, WRONG NUMBER, or NOT INTERESTED immediately halts all automated messages.

Our system records this status in real-time, suppresses automated scheduled follow-ups, and archives the conversation.

thumb_up 4. Message Quality, Rate Limits & Frequency

To prevent notification fatigue and maintain a superior Meta Quality Rating:

  • Strict Rate Limiting: We never broadcast bulk blasts. Automated outreach is paced with intelligent delays (minimum 30–60 seconds between deliveries).
  • Maximum Outreach Attempts: If a lead does not engage after an initial audit proposal and scheduled follow-up, communications cease automatically.
  • Relevant Content: Messages are customized specifically to the recipient's website diagnostics, rather than generic spam templates.

schedule 5. 24-Hour Customer Care Window

When a lead replies to our message, Meta's 24-hour customer service window opens. During this period, our autonomous AI assistant promptly answers questions regarding website cost, portfolio examples, timeline, and design aesthetics.

Once the 24-hour window expires, free-form messaging terminates, and only Meta-approved customer care update templates may be utilized.

warning 6. Prohibited Messaging Categories

In accordance with WhatsApp Commerce and Messaging Policies, our platform strictly prohibits:

  • Transmission of deceptive or misleading commercial representations.
  • Promotion of prohibited categories (e.g., pharmaceuticals, weapons, adult entertainment, illegal gambling).
  • Harassment, repetitive unwanted pings, or abusive language.

person_pin 7. Human Escalation Support

If at any point during a conversation you prefer to speak directly with a human team member:

Simply type TALK TO HUMAN or ADMIN. Our AI assistant will immediately transfer the thread to our lead engineering team, and an administrator will respond directly from our verified WhatsApp business desk.
